# Иконка QR Duplicator

Создана встроенным imagegen, прозрачный фон. PNG: `public/assets/app-icon.png`. ICO: `build/icon.ico`, упакован штатным преобразователем electron-builder, размеры 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 px.

## Prompt

Create a single polished Windows desktop application icon for QR Duplicator, on a truly transparent background. Square 1:1 composition, icon fills about 88 percent of canvas with small even transparent margins. Two overlapping thick rounded-square glass tiles, rear tile shifted to upper-right in soft lavender, front tile centered slightly lower-left in icy cyan and pale blue glass. Crisp dark navy outline accents and bright glass edge highlights. On the FRONT tile place a very simplified bold navy QR glyph: three large square finder motifs in top-left, top-right, bottom-left and only four chunky modules bottom-right. The motif is an icon, not a functional barcode. Front-facing almost flat perspective for clarity. High contrast, restrained glossy reflection, no thin fussy details, no tiny squares, no huge shadow, no scenery, no text, no letters, no branding, no pedestal. The silhouette and copy metaphor must read clearly at 32x32 pixels. Premium clean glass look consistent with a pale cyan-lavender desktop interface. Output just the icon with alpha transparency.
